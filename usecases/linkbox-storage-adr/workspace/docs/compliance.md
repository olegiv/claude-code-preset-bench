# Data-handling rules for linkbox

The parts of the company's data-handling policy that apply to an internal service storing what its users save.

1. The links a user saves are personal data.
2. Personal data stays in the EU: store it only in the `eu-west` or `eu-central` region.
3. When a user's account is closed, their personal data must be erased within 30 days, backups included. Account
   closures reach the owning team as a ticket.
4. Personal data at rest must be encrypted.
5. A service that stores personal data must keep backups that can restore it after an operator's mistake.
